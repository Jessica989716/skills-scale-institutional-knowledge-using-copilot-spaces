## Summary of OctoAcme project management processes

OctoAcme follows an iterative, customer-first project management approach. Work begins with a lightweight initiation phase that includes a one-pager, stakeholder alignment, and clear objectives. From there, teams move into planning, where backlog items, estimates, definitions of done, and risk considerations are clarified before execution begins.

During execution, delivery happens in short increments with an established cadence, including standups, weekly syncs, and demos to keep stakeholders informed and aligned. Releases follow a checklist-driven process with CI validation, smoke tests, and rollback planning to reduce operational risk. Continuous improvement is captured through retrospectives that track action items and reinforce learning across teams.

Risks, dependencies, and communication paths are managed through a structured risk register and defined escalation practices, ensuring that issues are surfaced early and addressed with the right stakeholders. Roles and responsibilities are clearly defined so that accountability stays consistent throughout initiation, planning, execution, release, and review.
